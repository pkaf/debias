FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with deBias.

What makes a deBias algorithm audit different?
We conduct independent end-to-end reviews covering data lineage, model architecture, decision thresholds, and governance policies. Deliverables include severity-ranked findings and practical remediation steps that stand up to executive and regulatory scrutiny.
How long does a typical consultancy or audit engagement take?
Standard engagements run from 2 to 6 weeks depending on scope, model complexity, and team size. We operate on a fixed-charter structure with weekly milestones so your team always has complete visibility.
Can training programs be customized for our organization?
Yes. Our business training workshops range from executive AI literacy briefings to intensive technical upskilling, fully customized around your company’s tech stack, data culture, and industry domain.
